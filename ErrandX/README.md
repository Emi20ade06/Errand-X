## 🌟 Overview
ErrandX is a campus-first peer-to-peer service marketplace built specifically for Bowen University. It matches students who need services with verified student operators capable of delivering high-quality tasks. Every transaction is covered under a custom trust-verified, secure escrow hold layer utilizing simulated Paystack API holds.

---

## 🛠️ Service Sectors
Our on-demand campus services are tailored to peak student needs:
1. **📚 Calculus & Study Tutorials** — Direct peer revision and exam preps.
2. **👚 Hostel Laundry & Ironing** — Express laundry pickups from halls of residence.
3. **💻 Hardware & Laptop Repair** — Software reinstalls, screen fixes, and maintenance.
4. **🍳 Meal Prep & Food Delivery** — Hot meals prepped and delivered directly to hostels.

---

## 🔒 Protection Layers
Trust is our highest priority. ErrandX includes four custom-built security systems:
- 💳 **Secure Paystack Escrow Hold**: All funds are securely frozen in escrow. The operator only receives payment when the client marks the job as completed.
- 📱 **OTP Live Mobile Phone Match**: Instant peer verification upon arrival to prevent service spoofing.
- 🎓 **Verified Student Matric Database**: Only registered Bowen University students with valid credentials can become active operators.
- ⚖️ **24-Hour Arbitration Dispute Panel**: 24-hour dispute window managed by a dedicated student-moderator panel to resolve conflicts.

## 📥 How to Run
This directory contains a **fully self-contained, offline-first, mobile-responsive web version** of ErrandX:
1. Simply double-click on `index.html` to open it in any browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge).
2. No internet, compilation, or `npm install` are required for the static version—making it perfect for slides, offline presentations, or direct grading.
3. Key visual modal windows (Login, Signup, Booking, and Rating) have been centered and responsive-adjusted for seamless display on both phones and desktops.
